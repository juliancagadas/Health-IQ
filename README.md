# Health-IQ
A Fuzzy Logic-Based Health Assessment and Recommendation System built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

<br>

## How to Use HealthIQ ?

### 1. Open HealthIQ
Launch the HealthIQ application and go to the **Health Assessment** page.

### 2. Enter Your Vital Signs
Adjust the sliders based on your current condition:

- **Body Temperature** – set your current temperature in °C.
- **Heart Rate** – set your current heart rate in BPM.

### 3. Rate Your Symptoms
Use the sliders to rate the severity of your symptoms from **0 to 10**.

The symptoms include:

- Headache
- Cough
- Fatigue
- Nausea
- Sore Throat
- Difficulty Breathing

### 4. Click **Analyze Health**
After entering your information, click the **Analyze Health** button.

HealthIQ will process your inputs using **fuzzy logic**, including:

- Fuzzification
- Fuzzy rule evaluation
- Risk classification

### 5. View Your Results
The system will display:

- **Final Risk Level** – Low, Moderate, or High Risk
- **Risk Strength** – the calculated fuzzy risk value
- **Health Analysis**
- **Recommendations**

### 6. Read the Recommendations
HealthIQ provides:

- **General recommendations** based on your final risk level.
- **Rule-specific recommendations** based on the strongest activated fuzzy rule.

> **Disclaimer:** HealthIQ is a school project that demonstrates the application of fuzzy logic in health assessment. It is intended for educational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
